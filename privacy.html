Privacy Policy

What the Reelish service stores for your account, what stays on your device, and what is shared with the services you use.

Last updated November 15, 2026

This is the same privacy policy shown in the Reelish app before first use. A section on this website is added at the end.

Who this applies to

This policy describes the Reelish app and the Reelish API, the online service the app uses for accounts, the catalog and watch parties. To browse the catalog you sign in with a Trakt account; Reelish has no passwords of its own. Your device and the services you choose to use also process data as described below.

Your Reelish account

You sign in on Trakt’s own page in your browser, or by entering a code on Trakt’s website from a TV. Reelish never sees your Trakt password. When you sign in, the Reelish API stores your Trakt account ID and username; your Trakt access tokens, encrypted, so the service can reach Trakt for you; and, for each signed-in device, a general label (such as “Android phone” or “Windows”), when it signed in and was last used, and session tokens kept only in hashed form. The service does not ask for your email address, contacts, payment details or precise location. Trakt’s own privacy policy applies to your Trakt account.

Viewing progress across your devices

So you can stop on one device and continue on another, the Reelish API stores, for your account, where you left each movie and episode you play: its TMDB number, the position and length, the next episode once one is finished, whether you removed it from Continue watching, and the title, year and TMDB artwork addresses its card shows. Each entry also holds a random identifier made by the app for each device (not a hardware identifier) and when the change was made. Nothing about the stream or provider you used is sent. Entries unchanged for over a year are deleted, and all of them when you delete your account.

Your collections across your devices

So your collections are the same on each device, the Reelish API stores, for your account, the folders and collections you make (their names, descriptions, order and chosen cover) and the titles in each: TMDB number, title, year, TMDB artwork addresses and when it was added. Each entry also holds the random device identifier described above and when the change was made. A deletion is kept as a short mark, with no name or title, for a year so your other devices remove the item too, then deleted. Everything is deleted when you delete your account.

Recommendation preferences

So recommendations follow you to each device, the Reelish API stores, for your account, the genres you pick, whether you answered or skipped the genre question, and whether personalised rows are on, with the random device identifier described above and when the change was made. What you watch and your feedback on recommendations are worked out on each device and not sent for this. They are deleted when you delete your account.

Friends

Friends is off until you turn it on in Profile > Friends & privacy. Then the Reelish API stores, for your account, the handle you choose, a random friend code, and, only if you set them, a name and one of the built-in avatars for friends to see, along with your Friends privacy settings. It also stores your friendships, friend requests waiting for an answer (deleted after 30 days) and the people you blocked. When you share a title, the friends you choose receive its title, year, TMDB artwork address and your note, kept for 180 days or until they remove it; it stays with them if you stop being friends, and blocking removes it. A collection you share with friends (every collection is private until you do) shows them its name, description and titles; making it private again, removing a friend, blocking or turning Friends off hides it at once. Only if you turn on "Share what I'm watching", the title you are playing (and its season and episode) is sent when it starts, for your friends to see; it is removed when you stop, expires on its own within hours even if the app closes unexpectedly, is never kept as a history, and is not sent during a Watch Party. Others can find you only by your exact friend code, or your exact handle if you allow it, and see only your handle and what you chose to share. A report you make about someone is kept with bug reports for up to 180 days, with the reported account and without your account id. Your Trakt username, Trakt picture and any profile photo are never shown to other people. Turning Friends off deletes all of this at once; your handle is then held for 30 days, with nothing else, so nobody else can take it and appear to be you, then released. Everything is deleted when you delete your account.

Information kept on your device

The app stores your favorites, recently played titles and resume positions, playback and notification preferences, your profile display name and avatar choice, installed add-on repository addresses and settings, a short-lived cache of selected stream sources, and a copy of the plugin library catalog in the app’s private storage, and your sign-in session in the device’s secure storage. A profile photo you choose stays on the device and is never uploaded. The app has no advertising. Usage statistics are sent only if you agree (see Usage statistics). You can remove favorites and history in Library, remove add-ons in Plugins, clear the torrent cache in Playback settings, or erase app data by uninstalling the app. Device backups and operating-system behavior are controlled by your platform provider.

Information sent when you use network features

When you browse, search or open titles, the app sends title identifiers and related requests to the Reelish API, which retrieves catalog data and details from The Movie Database (TMDB) for you; TMDB receives those requests from the service, not from your device. Artwork loads directly from TMDB’s image servers. If you turn on My list sync, the service reads and changes your Trakt watchlist for you. If you turn on new-episode alerts, the app sends the TMDB identifiers of series on My list or in progress to the service to check for new episodes; they are not stored. In a watch party, the title, the playback position and play or pause state, and each participant’s display name, chosen preset avatar (never a photo) and player status are shared through the service with the people in that party. Subtitle searches send a title or episode identifier to the OpenSubtitles v3 service. Intro and outro skipping sends an episode’s IMDb and episode numbers to IntroDB; turn both off in Settings > Playback to send nothing. Opening the plugin library downloads its catalog from the app’s GitHub repository and loads provider logos from the addresses listed in it; those hosts can receive your IP address and request data, but no title or account information. If you install an add-on, the app contacts the repository and service addresses configured for it. Add-ons receive the title and episode identifiers needed for your request and may send them to their own servers. Direct streams, subtitle files and torrent/P2P playback connect to the selected source or peers; those operators can receive your IP address and request data. Video never passes through the Reelish API. Android and Windows may contact GitHub to check for app updates. When you choose an update, Android downloads an APK and uses its package installer after you confirm; installed Windows copies download the installer and run it after checking it, and portable copies download a ZIP for you to extract.

Problem reports and feedback

When you send a report or feedback from Help & Support, the Reelish API stores what you wrote (title, category, details and optional steps), the device type you chose, and, unless you turn it off, the app version, build, operating system and version, interface, language and time. You see all of it before sending. It does not include your account name, history, providers or logs; the report is linked to a one-way code derived from your account so a person can send a limited number per day and so deleting your account deletes your reports. Reports are deleted after 180 days. Do not include personal information you do not want stored.

Usage statistics (Android only, optional)

On Android phones, Android TV and Google TV, Reelish asks once whether you want to share usage statistics, and sends none until you agree. If you do, Google Analytics for Firebase receives which pages and features you use, whether a search found results (not what you searched for), and for each playback session the movie or episode’s public TMDB number, how long it took to start, buffering, the kind of error if it failed, and roughly how much was watched. It never receives your account, Trakt name, display name, search text, add-on or provider names, stream addresses or tokens. Google also records standard app information such as device model, operating-system version, app version, approximate country and an app-instance identifier; the advertising identifier, the Android ID and ad personalization are turned off. Google keeps this data under its own policies and Reelish’s retention setting. You can turn sharing off in Settings at any time; doing so also clears the analytics data and identifier stored on the device. Windows sends no usage statistics.

Crash reports (Android only, optional)

On Android phones, Android TV and Google TV, Reelish asks separately whether you want to share crash reports, and sends none until you agree. If you do, Firebase Crashlytics receives what went wrong: the type of error and where in the app’s code it happened, a short description with addresses, tokens and codes removed, which page was open, whether a video or watch party was active and in which state, and recent app events that led up to it. If you also share usage statistics, those events can appear with the report. Google also records standard details such as device model, operating-system version, app version, free memory and storage, and an installation identifier. Reports never include your account, Trakt name, display name, search text, add-on or provider names, stream addresses, tokens or watch party codes. Google keeps reports for 90 days. You can turn sharing off in Settings at any time; doing so also deletes reports not yet sent.

Error log (Windows)

On Windows, Reelish keeps a small log of app errors on your computer to help with troubleshooting. It is limited in size, has addresses, tokens and codes removed, and is never sent anywhere. You can open it from Help & Support.

Supporting Reelish (optional)

Donations are made on Buy Me a Coffee, which handles your payment and your details under its own policies; Reelish never sees your card. When you donate, Buy Me a Coffee tells the Reelish API that a donation succeeded. If your message contains your supporter code, your account receives the Supporter Badge. Reelish keeps only a one-way reference to the donation, its date and whether it was refunded, and not the amount, your name, your email address or your message. Your badge and the date you became a supporter are stored with your account and are deleted with it. A refunded donation no longer counts. You appear on the supporters list only if you turn that on, under a name you choose, which anyone using Reelish can see; you can turn it off at any time. If you ask for a review by email, the email is used only to check your donation.

Third-party services and add-ons

Trakt, TMDB, Cloudflare (which hosts the Reelish API), Google (usage statistics and crash reports, only if you agree), Buy Me a Coffee (only if you donate), OpenSubtitles, IntroDB, add-on publishers, media hosts, torrent peers, GitHub, and your device platform operate independently. Their privacy practices, logs, retention and locations are governed by their own policies. Cloudflare processes your IP address and request data to deliver the Reelish API. The app cannot control what an add-on publisher does with data it receives. Install only add-ons you trust. No payment, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier is requested by the app’s own features.

Retention and choices

Your account data stays on the Reelish API until you delete your account. You can sign out of one device or of all devices, and delete your account in Settings > Profile and account; deleting it asks Trakt to revoke Reelish’s access and removes your account, your viewing progress, collections and recommendation preferences, your encrypted Trakt tokens and your device sessions, including those already signed out. A minimal security log, holding an internal account number, the kind of event (such as a sign-in or a sign-out) and its time, but no username, title or viewing information, is kept for 12 months to detect misuse, including after an account is deleted, and then erased. A watch party’s room is deleted when the party ends, and after 12 hours at most. Operational logs of the service record which address was requested and when, which can include a title identifier but not your account name, and are kept by the hosting provider for a limited period. On-device library entries and preferences remain until you remove them or erase app data. Third-party services may retain request data under their own policies; for data they hold, contact that service or add-on publisher directly. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location; email the developer at frnzgularez@gmail.com for requests concerning the app or the Reelish API.

Children and changes

The app is not designed as a service for children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information to a third-party service should contact that service and the app publisher. This policy may change when app features or legal requirements change; the updated version and date will appear here.

This website

Advertising. This website shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and measure them, and these ads can be based on your visits to this website and to other websites. You can turn off personalized advertising in Google’s Ad Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. The Reelish app itself has no advertising.

The website has no analytics of its own. To show the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page and changelog load release information, including download counts, from the GitHub API. Those services, and the host serving this site, can receive your IP address and standard request data under their own policies. Download links take you to GitHub Releases.

Reviews. The review form on the home page is optional. It sends your rating, display name, platform and review text to Netlify, which hosts this website and stores form submissions; Netlify also records the submission time and your IP address and may check submissions for spam. The developer reads each submission and may publish the rating, display name, platform, date and text on this website if you agreed to that. Not every review is published. To have your review removed from the website or deleted from Netlify, email frnzgularez@gmail.com with your display name and roughly when you submitted it.

Contact

For privacy requests, email the developer at frnzgularez@gmail.com. Do not post personal information in public issue trackers.
