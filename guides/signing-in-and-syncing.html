Signing In and Syncing

Sign in with Trakt and keep Reelish the same on every device.

2 min read

Reelish uses your Trakt account to sign in. Reelish has no passwords of its own and never sees your Trakt password. If you don’t have a Trakt account, you can create one for free on Trakt’s sign-in page.

Sign in on a phone, tablet or PC

  1. On Sign in to Reelish, tap Continue with Trakt.
  2. Your browser opens Trakt’s page. Sign in and approve Reelish.
  3. Come back to Reelish. The page updates by itself and shows Signed in. Loading Reelish….

If your browser didn’t open, tap Open Trakt again, or Use a code instead.

Sign in on a TV, or with a code

  1. On a TV, choose Sign in with Trakt. Elsewhere, tap Sign in with a code instead.
  2. On your phone or computer, go to the address shown and Enter this code.
  3. Approve Reelish. The TV continues by itself.

If the code runs out, tap Get a new code.

What syncs between your devices

  • Continue watching: where you left each movie and episode, and the next episode to watch.
  • Collections and folders you create.
  • Genres you love and whether personal rows are on.
  • My list, if you turn on Sync My list with Trakt watchlist in Settings → Profile and account. It then stays in step with your Trakt watchlist.
  • Your Trakt avatar and username.

A display name, avatar or photo you choose in Reelish stays on that device, and a photo is never uploaded. Favorites, settings and plugins stay on each device. Nothing about the providers or streams you use is sent.

Sign out or delete your account

Open Settings → Profile and account:

  • Sign out signs this device out. Sign out everywhere signs out all your devices.
  • Delete account removes your account, viewing progress, collections, recommendation preferences and device sessions from the Reelish service, and asks Trakt to revoke Reelish’s access.

See the privacy policy for exactly what’s stored and for how long.
