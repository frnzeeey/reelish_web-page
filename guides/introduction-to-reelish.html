Getting Started

Introduction to Reelish

An overview of what Reelish is and how it works.

2 min read

Reelish is an open-source app for discovering movies and TV series and watching them with the providers you choose. It runs on Android phones and tablets, Android TV, Google TV and Windows, and combines a polished catalog, a personal library that follows you across your devices, a built-in video player and ways to watch with friends.

How Reelish works

Reelish splits the job of “finding something to watch” and “playing it” into separate pieces:

  • The catalog comes from TMDB. Titles, artwork, synopses, genres, cast and people are loaded from The Movie Database through the Reelish service.
  • Your account comes from Trakt. You sign in with a free Trakt account, and your viewing progress, collections and favorite genres stay the same on every device you use.
  • Streams come from providers you install. When you press play, Reelish asks your enabled providers for playable sources for that title. Providers don’t add titles to the catalog; they only answer “where can this be played?”.
  • Playback happens in the built-in player. It supports direct HTTP(S) streams, external subtitles, audio languages, intro skipping and more. On Android and Windows, compatible providers can also return torrent sources for P2P playback.

What Reelish doesn’t do

Reelish does not host, store or distribute any media, and video never passes through the Reelish service. Providers are third-party code published by independent authors, and the sources they return are operated by others. Install plugin collections only from publishers you trust.

What leaves your device

Your viewing progress, collections and genre choices are stored with your account so they sync. Favorites, settings and provider choices stay on the device, and nothing about the providers or streams you use is sent. See the privacy policy for the details.

What you need

  • An Android phone, tablet, Android TV or Google TV device, or a Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit).
  • The latest release from GitHub Releases.
  • A free Trakt account to sign in.
  • At least one plugin collection, added from the Plugins tab.

Where to go next

Start with Downloading the APK and Installing on Android, then Signing In and Syncing. Read Understanding Third-Party Plugins before you add your first provider.
