Getting Started

Navigating the App

Learn how to find your way around the Reelish interface.

3 min read

On phones and tablets, Reelish is organized around four tabs in the dock at the bottom of the screen: Home, Library, Plugins and Settings. Your Profile opens from your avatar at the top right of Home. On Android TV and Google TV the same places are in the side menu, and on Windows in the sidebar; both also have Movies, Series and Search.

Home

Home is where discovery happens.

  • Reelish Spotlight: a rotating feature at the top, on phones and Windows. Swipe through it, open the title or add it to My list. Choose how it moves in Settings → Appearance → Spotlight Experience.
  • Join party and Search: on phones, at the top of Home next to your avatar.
  • Category chips: switch between For you, Movies, Series and the other categories to change what the rows below show.
  • Rows such as Top 10 picks, Continue watching, Trending and New releases, plus rows picked for you: Recommended for you, Because you watched a title, Because you like a genre, and Something different. Turn the personal rows off in Settings → Recommendations.
  • Search: one search finds movies, series, people and movie collections. Filter movies and series by genre, year and rating.

New accounts are asked What do you love watching?. The genres you pick shape your recommendations and follow your account to your other devices.

Title details

Opening a title shows its artwork, rating, synopsis, genres and cast. For movies, tap Play movie; for series, pick a season and an episode. If you’ve started it, choose Resume or Start over. Reelish then searches your enabled providers for a stream. From the same page you can add the title to My list or a collection, Share it with friends, or start a Watch Party with Watch together.

Tap a cast member to see what they acted in and directed. A movie that belongs to a series of films links to its collection, shown in release order. Both have a Follow button so you hear when there’s something new.

Library

My library keeps everything you are watching and saving in one place. Use the Everything, Continue, My list and Collections filters to narrow it down.

  • Continue watching: titles you paused, on any of your devices. Finished titles leave the row, and a series shows its next episode.
  • My list: titles you saved for another night.
  • Collections: your own lists and folders, plus smart collections that fill themselves. See Library and Collections.
  • Recently watched: your latest activity. You can remove items from your watch history.

Plugins

The Plugins tab manages where streams come from. You can browse the community library, add a collection by link, see your active sources and turn individual providers on or off. See Managing Providers.

Profile

Your profile shows your avatar, name, badges and your viewing at a glance (watched, in progress and on My list). From it you can open Friends, Viewing statistics and Reelish Wrapped, and change your Profile card, Theme and, on Android, App icon.

Settings

Settings is where you make Reelish work the way you like:

  • Profile and account: your display name and avatar, My list sync with Trakt, signing out and deleting your account.
  • Appearance: the theme, accent color, the Spotlight animation (Spotlight Experience) and, on Android, the app icon.
  • Notifications: new episodes, updates to titles on My list, people and collections you follow, and how alerts arrive.
  • Recommendations: the personal rows on Home.
  • Join a watch party: enter a party code.
  • Playback: player, stream selection, intro skipping, audio, subtitles and P2P options.
  • Subtitle addons: the subtitle sources searched while you watch.
  • Help & Support: answers, troubleshooting, problem reports, release notes, About Reelish (version and updates), Reelish Supporters, and the privacy policy and terms of use.
