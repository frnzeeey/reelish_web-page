Troubleshooting

Common Errors

Quick reference for frequent error messages.

2 min read

A quick reference for messages you might see in Reelish, what they mean and how to fix them.

Catalog

MessageFix
Could not load titles. Check your connection and retry.Check your connection, then tap Retry.
The catalog is busy right now. Wait a moment and retry.Wait a few seconds before retrying.

Sign-in

MessageFix
That code expired.Tap Get a new code and enter the new one on Trakt.
Sign-in was declined on Trakt.Tap Try again and approve Reelish on Trakt’s page.
Sign-in is not available right now.Trakt may be busy. Try again in a few minutes.
Your browser didn’t openTap Open Trakt again, or Use a code instead.
Your session could not be verified. Sign out and in again.Sign out in Settings → Profile and account, then sign in again.

Watch Party

MessageFix
No party has that code. Check it and try again.Ask the host for the code again; it stops working when the party ends.
This party is full.A party holds up to five people, including the host.
Hosting a Watch Party is for Reelish Supporters.Join a friend’s party with a code, or see Reelish Supporters.
Too many tries. Wait a minute and try again.Wait a minute before entering the code again.

More detail in Watch Party.

Providers

MessageFix
No providers are installed.Add a repository — see Adding Plugin Repositories.
Your installed providers are switched off.Enable at least one provider in Plugins.
No provider plugins are allowed for automatic stream selection.Update Settings → Playback → Allowed plugins.
The enabled providers returned no streams for this title.Try another title, or enable more providers.
This provider took too long to respond.Try again later or use another provider.

Playback

MessageFix
Unable to play this sourceTap Try another source.
This device could not decode the video format.Use Switch to … or a lower Quality.
The stream took too long to start.Check your connection or pick another source.
Provider returned an expired or invalid source.Play again to get a fresh link.

More detail in Playback Failures.

Subtitles

MessageFix
No external subtitles foundTry another language or add a subtitle addon.
Subtitles are busy. Try again in a moment.Wait a few seconds, then retry.
This subtitle format is not supported.Choose a different subtitle track.

Updates

MessageFix
Unable to check for updatesCheck your connection and retry from Settings → Help & Support → About Reelish.
The downloaded update failed its integrity check.Retry the download.
Allow Reelish to install updatesTap Open settings and allow installs from Reelish.
