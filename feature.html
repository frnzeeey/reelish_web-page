Features

Built around your viewing experience.

Explore the features that make Reelish a flexible and enjoyable entertainment companion.

Movie & TV Discovery

Explore a rich catalog of movies and series powered by TMDB. One search finds movies, series, people and movie collections. Open an actor’s page to see what they acted in and directed, or browse a film collection in release order.

  • Top 10 picks, trending and new releases
  • Filter by genre, year and rating
  • Pages for people and movie collections
  • See which of your providers have a movie

Android TV & Google TV

Take Reelish to the living room. The same APK you install on your phone also runs on Android TV and Google TV, and the TV layout is designed for the big screen and a remote, with a side rail for navigation and large artwork that’s easy to browse from across the room.

  • Remote and D-pad navigation
  • Big-screen layout with side rail
  • Large artwork and readable text
  • One APK for phone, Android TV & Google TV

Personalized Recommendations

Tell Reelish the genres you love, and Home fills with rows picked for you: Recommended for you, Because you watched, Because you like, and Something different. Save titles to My list, group them into your own collections, and pick up where you left off on any device.

  • Personal rows on Home
  • My list and your own collections
  • Continue watching across devices
  • Turn personal rows off anytime

Third-Party Plugin Support

Extend Reelish’s reach by connecting compatible third-party content providers. Install plugins from the community library or by link, and give each provider its own page with its source, version and last error. Updates wait for your review before anything changes. Providers are independently operated.

  • Community library and add by link
  • Enable / disable per source
  • Updates held for your review
  • No lock-in to a single provider

Video Playback Experience

A full-featured video player built for comfort. Intros skip themselves, audio tracks are named by their language, and speed, quality, subtitles and picture are a tap away. If a source fails, Reelish reconnects or tries another one.

  • Skip intro and outro
  • Audio & language by name
  • Multi-source stream selection
  • Picture in picture on Android
Subtitles, styled the way you like.

Subtitle Customization

Make subtitles work for your environment. Adjust font size, color, background opacity, and vertical position, and fix timing in a few steps, to create a comfortable reading experience regardless of viewing conditions.

  • Font size and style
  • Color and background options
  • Timing and vertical position
  • Subtitle language selection

App Personalization

Make Reelish yours. Pick any accent color, dress your profile card, and choose how and when Reelish tells you about new episodes. Supporters also get app themes, including the light Ivory Studio, plus avatar frames.

  • Accent colors and app themes
  • Profile card and avatar frames
  • Notifications with quiet hours
  • Playback defaults

Better together

Watch with friends. Pick up anywhere.

Sign in with your free Trakt account and Reelish follows you from phone to TV to PC, with ways to share what you love.

Watch Party

Watch in sync with up to five people, each from their own sources. React and chat on top of the video, and share an invite link that opens Reelish with the code filled in.

Friends & Messages

Add friends by code or handle, send them titles to watch, share your collections, and message them privately: unsend a message, set nicknames and mute a conversation. Off until you turn it on.

Continue Anywhere

Your progress, collections and favorite genres follow your account. Stop on one device and pick up on another, with the next episode ready as Up next.

Reelish Wrapped

Your year on Reelish, told as a story: what you finished, your watch time, top genres and actors, and your viewing personality. Turn any slide into a card to share.

Smarter Notifications

Hear about new episodes and seasons, release dates, and new work from people and collections you follow. Get them as they happen or as a summary, with quiet hours.

Reelish Supporters

Donating is optional. As a thank-you, Supporters can host Watch Parties and choose app themes, avatar frames, extra Spotlight animations and every app icon. Everything else is free for everyone.
