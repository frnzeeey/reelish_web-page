Features

Built around your viewing experience.

Explore the features that make Reelish a flexible and enjoyable entertainment companion.

Movie & TV Discovery

Explore a rich catalog of movies and series powered by TMDB. Browse trending titles, new releases, top-rated content, and curated categories designed to surface what you actually want to watch.

  • Trending and popular content
  • Genre and category browsing
  • TMDB-powered metadata
  • Search across titles

Android TV & Google TV

Take Reelish to the living room. The same APK you install on your phone also runs on Android TV and Google TV, and the TV layout is designed for the big screen and a remote, with a side rail for navigation and large artwork that’s easy to browse from across the room.

  • Remote and D-pad navigation
  • Big-screen layout with side rail
  • Large artwork and readable text
  • One APK for phone, Android TV & Google TV

Personalized Recommendations

Reelish learns from your preferences over time, surfacing titles more relevant to your taste. Manage your watchlist, track what you’ve seen, and get suggestions tailored to you.

  • Watchlist management
  • Viewing history tracking
  • Personal taste signals
  • Flexible preference controls

Third-Party Plugin Support

Extend Reelish’s reach by connecting compatible third-party content providers. Add, manage, and toggle plugins from within the app. Reelish gives you the control — providers are independently operated.

  • Plugin repository support
  • Enable / disable per source
  • Plugin health indicators
  • No lock-in to a single provider

Video Playback Experience

A full-featured video player built for comfort. Control playback speed, resize video to fill the screen, and choose between available stream sources — all from an uncluttered interface.

  • Playback speed control
  • Video scaling modes
  • Multi-source stream selection
  • Background audio support
Subtitles, styled the way you like.

Subtitle Customization

Make subtitles work for your environment. Adjust font size, color, background opacity, and vertical position to create a comfortable reading experience regardless of viewing conditions.

  • Font size and style
  • Color and background options
  • Vertical positioning
  • Subtitle language selection

App Personalization

Reelish puts thoughtful settings within reach. Adjust display preferences, notification behavior, and playback defaults to match how you use the app every day.

  • Display and theme options
  • Playback defaults
  • Notification preferences
  • Data and cache controls
